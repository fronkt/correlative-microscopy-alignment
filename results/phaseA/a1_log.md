# A1 log: the 42 confident TEM false accepts (exploratory; nothing here is confirmatory)

Script: `scripts/phaseA_a1_false_accepts.py` (run with `PYTHONPATH=src`; about 12 min, most of it the lattice permutations and 126 SIFT reruns). Outputs: `a1_false_accepts.json`, `a1_per_pair.csv`, plus `a1_geometry.csv`, `a1_candidates_vs_maroma.csv`, `a1_sift_crop_experiment.csv`.

## Definition and reproduction
- MA-RoMa, core, direct, transform none, seed 0. Held-out groups = everything except SameSlice and SerialSectioning. Youden cut-off on the two design groups recomputed: 0.1711. Rule: S1 >= cut and mu_ed > 20.
- Asserted: 42 rows, all DislocationCharacterization, all MoTaTiZrHf, 3 scenes (27 / 1 / 14 pairs). Matches E7 in exploratory.json (42, err min 20.69, max 526.9).
- H convention read from `run_triage_candidates.py`: H maps TARGET to SOURCE; mu_ed = mean |H(tgt_gt) - src_gt|. H_rel = H_gt @ inv(H_est) acts in the source frame (estimated position -> true position). Translation is taken at the target centre, checked against a direct H_gt(c) - H_est(c) (assert passes).

## Variants tried (all of them)
1. (i) "same wrong" = other candidate's H within 20 px of MA-RoMa's H (mean over a 5x5 grid on the target) and itself > 20 px. Single variant. Weakness: for the 8 near-misses (20-30 px) "same wrong" is nearly automatic. I did not build a null for it.
2. (ii) decomposition about the projected target centre using the local Jacobian for rotation/scale/anisotropy/shear (SVD polar split); perspective = |(h31, h32)|. Translation share = 1 - (mean residual after removing the mean displacement on the 5x5 grid) / (mean displacement).
3. Clustering of translations: single linkage at 20 px and 50 px.
4. (iii) autocorrelation. Dead end 1: my first version normalised by the Hann-window overlap and picked local maxima with float64 vs float32 equality, so it returned garbage peaks at extreme lags (heights above 1). Dead end 2: after fixing dtype, dividing by overlap still amplified noise at large lags, so I required overlap >= 30% of maximum and |lag| <= half the image. Dead end 3: I first analysed only pair 0's images per scene; the 42 pairs use 8 distinct source images, so the final version uses each pair's own source image. Final variants: high-pass (Gaussian sigma 8 px at 512) at 512 (primary), raw at 512, high-pass at 256, high-pass at 1024. Peaks: top 3 non-origin (one of each +/- pair), z-scored against the valid-lag distribution. The target-image variant was dropped from the final script because source and target pixel sizes are identical (scale about 1.00, Jacobian about identity), so target periods equal source periods.
5. Lattice statistic: mean over pairs of the minimum distance from the error translation to {m g1 + n g2, |m|,|n| <= 6}. Nulls (1,000 draws): each source image's generator set rotated by a random common angle; and generators given independent random angles, lengths kept. Per-scene tests use 300 draws and an independent random rotation per pair (not per source image), a slight inconsistency with the pooled test. With fewer than 2 peaks, the lattice degenerates to 1-D or the origin.
6. Identity probe (unplanned): distance of H_est to the identity map on the 5x5 grid.
7. SIFT probe on 5 pairs (one per scene x GT-shift configuration): raw matches, fraction with |a-b| < 5 px, fraction consistent with the GT-fit H (< 10 px), where the near-zero-displacement matches sit.
8. SIFT crop experiment on all 42 pairs: crop 0, 9 % and 15 % of the height off the bottom of both images, refit with the base pipeline (family auto, RANSAC 5.5 px, same per-pair seed). Crop 0 reproduces the stored SIFT error to within 1 px on 42/42 pairs.

## Results
(iv) Thresholds: 8 pairs at 20-30 px; 37 stay failures at 25 px, 34 at 30 px; 27 exceed 100 px (and 150 px). GT-fit homography error < 20 px on 41/42 (claim confirmed); the exception is 900C_TEM_DislocationCharacterization_0#0 at 29.4 px (it is one of the 20-30 px group, err 53.6). GT-fit affine is < 20 px on only 32/42, so the model family matters for the affine fits (27 of the 42 MA-RoMa fits were affine, 15 homography).

(i) Shared vs matcher-specific (1,008 other-candidate rows): SAME-WRONG 525, ELSEWHERE 360, CORRECT 123. CORRECT comes only from RoMa (113) and MA-RoMa transforms (10); SIFT, LoFTR, MatchAnything and MA-RoMa seeds/pyramid never reach the truth on any of the 42. Same-wrong by source: MA-RoMa pyramid 42/42, MA-RoMa seeds 177/210, SIFT 29/42, MatchAnything 29/42, LoFTR 21/42, RoMa seeds 62/210 (RoMa direct 11/42 plus 10 correct). 22/42 pairs have at least one correct candidate; 19/42 have a majority of others at the same wrong place. In 39/42 pairs the largest cluster of the other candidates sits at a wrong place.

(ii) Geometry: mean translation about 220 px (median 168; 18-527 px), about 5.6 % of the source diagonal. Rotation median 0.46 deg (max 2.2), scale 0.967-1.017, anisotropy median 1.014 (max 1.074), shear max 0.035, perspective about 4e-6 (negligible). The translation accounts for a median 78 % of the grid displacement (15/42 above 90 %, 19/42 above 80 %). It is lowest (about 25-35 %) for the mid group in scene 1 (40-70 px errors), where the anisotropy/scale terms are the same size as the translation; those pairs mostly have GT-fit error 8-13 px, so part of that residual is GT-fit imperfection. Translations cluster hard: 8 clusters at 20 px linkage (sizes 11, 8, 8, 6, 5, 2, 1, 1), 4 at 50 px. They are essentially the GT shifts of the target tile: (-20,-50), (-34,-166), (58,20), (111,502), (202,385) px. So the 42 rows are roughly 5 geometric configurations, not 42 independent events.

Mechanism found. For all 42 pairs source and target have the same size and pixel size. The MA-RoMa estimate lies within 20 px of the identity map on 29/42 pairs (31 within 30 px; median 11.9 px from identity), while the true GT shift is 54-511 px (median 174). Among all 66 same-size pairs (all TEM), 35 have an estimate near identity, 0 of those succeed, and the identity map itself would succeed on 0 (no same-size pair has a GT shift under 20 px). The images carry a burned-in metadata/scale-bar banner at the same bottom pixel rows in every file (viewed 3 pairs). SIFT rerun: 87-99.7 % of the raw matches have displacement under 5 px, and 100 % of those lie in the bottom 9 % of the image; almost none (0-1 matches) agree with the GT transform. With the bottom 9 % cropped, SIFT's estimates leave the identity on 42/42 pairs (near-identity count 42 -> 0), but SIFT still succeeds on 0/42, so the banner explains the identity attractor, not the wrongness that remains for SIFT. Not tested for the dense matchers (correspondences were not stored, GPU needed). The 13 pairs not near identity are mostly the near-misses (est close to GT). Note the GT point set reaches down to y = 0.92 of the height on some pairs, so a 9 % crop can cut the lowest GT rows, though this does not affect the error computation.

(iii) Repeating structure: no clear 2-D periodicity. The strongest autocorrelation peaks in several source images are horizontal-only lags (dy of 0-4 px), i.e. scan-line streaking, not a lattice; others have z about 4-7 at 1-2 image-widths of lag, unreliable. Lattice test: pooled observed mean distance 213 px vs null median 185 px (rotation null, p = 0.997) and 171 px (independent-angle null, p = 0.998); raw, 256 and 1024 variants give p of 0.96, 0.90, 0.88 (rotation) and 0.98, 0.92, 0.35 (independent). Per scene no p below 0.59. Translations are not closer to the lattices than random ones (if anything, farther). Caveat: peaks are unstable across variants, so this is a null result about these peak sets, not a proof of no periodicity.

## Surprises and contradictions with the plan text
- The dominant mechanism is not repeating structure or a matcher-specific error; it is a fixed-position artifact (metadata banner) that makes "no motion" a strong attractor for SIFT (verified) and, by the shared-H evidence, probably for the dense matchers too.
- "41/42" is confirmed, but for the homography fit only; the affine GT fit is under 20 px on 32/42.
- The false accepts are about 5 GT-shift configurations x modality combinations. Any statistic over "42" should be scene- or configuration-clustered.
- Step (i)'s 20 px "same wrong" is weak evidence for the near-miss pairs.
- Other Phase A files (a2-a5) in results/phaseA were written by other agents; untouched.
